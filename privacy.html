Oak Tree Road Passport

Privacy Notice

Last updated October 9, 2026

Who we are

The Oak Tree Road Passport is built and run by DotCode Networks for the Oak Tree Road businesses and the festival held with Woodbridge Township. Questions or requests: accounts@dotcodenetworks.com.

What we collect

Your name, and your mobile number or email. Which stores you scan and when. Whether you ticked each box when you signed up. Your phone's location at the moment of each scan, only to confirm you are on Oak Tree Road. A random code stored in your browser, to limit repeat sign-ups from one phone. We do not track you in the background.

Why we use it

To give you stamps and entries, run the prize drawings, and contact winners. To prevent cheating and duplicate sign-ups. To let the stores you scan honor their festival special.

Who sees it

The store you scan sees your name and contact details, and your scans at that store only. It does not see other stores' lists or where else you scanned. The festival organizers and DotCode see all entries so they can run the drawings. Service providers help us run the Passport: Supabase hosts our database, and Resend sends sign-in emails to store owners and organizers.

We do not sell your information. DotCode does not use it for its own marketing or for any other business or client.

Offers from stores: your choice

On sign-up there is an optional box: "the stores I scan may contact me about their festival offers and announcements." If you leave it unticked, stores can see that you scanned, but they are asked not to send you offers. If you tick it, only the stores you scanned may contact you, by text, WhatsApp, or email. You can opt out at any time by telling the store or emailing us.

How long we keep it

We will delete or anonymize visitor information by December 15, 2026, or sooner if you ask.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your information, or stop offers, at any time at accounts@dotcodenetworks.com.

Children

The Passport is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Security and changes

We protect data with access controls and encrypted connections. If we change this notice we will post the new version here with its date.
